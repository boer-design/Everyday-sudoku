Contact support

support@navero.app

Tell us what you were trying to do and what happened instead. It helps to include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The app version and, if available, the TestFlight build number.
  • The puzzle code from Progress → Recent puzzles, if your question concerns a puzzle.

A screenshot is welcome if it helps explain the issue. Please remove personal information first, and never send passwords or payment details.

Common questions

Can I play offline?

Yes. The puzzle catalogue and your current game are stored on your iPhone. Rewarded ads, purchases, restoring purchases and support email need an internet connection.

Where is my progress stored?

Your current game, puzzle history, XP and preferences are stored on your device. We do not keep a server copy of your puzzle progress. Deleting the app removes its local data unless it can be recovered from an applicable device backup.

How do I report a puzzle or hint problem?

Open Progress → Recent puzzles and include the puzzle code in your email. Describe the cell, entry or hint that seemed incorrect. You can also send a screenshot of the board.

How do hints work?

You receive three free hints each day. When you run out, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to refill three hints, or make a one-time Unlimited Hints purchase.

Hints explain the next logical number placement. Your pencil notes are personal and are not used to choose the hint. If an earlier mistake blocks progress, mistake recovery offers to rewind the puzzle to before that mistake.

Why is an ad unavailable?

An ad may be unavailable because of your connection, advertising choices or available ads. You can try again later or return to the puzzle. An unavailable ad leaves your hint balance unchanged and does not prevent ordinary play.

How do I restore Unlimited Hints?

Open Settings → Unlimited Hints → Restore Purchases with an internet connection and the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Unlimited Hints is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

If no purchase is found, check the Apple Account used for App Store purchases. If the restore option is missing or you still need help, contact us with your app version. Restoring a purchase restores paid access; it does not restore deleted puzzle history.

Where can I change advertising choices?

Open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review the available consent options. Apple’s tracking permission can also be changed in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
