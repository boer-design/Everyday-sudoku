# Puzzle beta privacy policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This policy applies to the Puzzle iPhone TestFlight beta provided by Navero App.

## Your game data

Puzzle stores your current and past games, progress, completion results, hint use, assistance settings, and board-style choice in a database on your device. The beta includes its puzzle catalogue in the app. We use this on-device data to resume games and show your progress. This beta has no player account or cloud sync. The app does not send your game progress or puzzle contents to a Puzzle server.

If the app or its local data is removed from your device, we do not have a server copy from which to restore your progress. Your device's backup settings may affect copies held in backups.

## TestFlight testing and feedback

Apple's TestFlight service distributes the beta. Apple automatically collects and shares crash logs and usage information with the app provider. TestFlight also provides device and build details and may show us your name and email address if you were invited by email. If you send feedback, you can add comments and choose whether to attach a screenshot. A screenshot may show the current puzzle or other content visible on your screen. Apple also receives TestFlight feedback and handles TestFlight data under [Apple's TestFlight privacy notice](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/).

We use TestFlight feedback to investigate beta issues and delete any reports or screenshots we download when they are no longer needed for that work. Apple's service retains its own copies under its terms.

## Direct support email

If you email support@navero.app, Navero App receives the email address you use and the message or attachments you choose to send. This inbox is shared across Navero App products. We use support messages to answer requests and identify recurring issues. We delete messages from our support inbox when you ask us to.

## Contact and changes

For a beta issue, use **Send Beta Feedback** on Puzzle's page in the TestFlight app or email [support@navero.app](mailto:support@navero.app). Use that address for support, privacy questions, or a request to delete a support message. Deleting a support email does not remove data held by Apple's TestFlight service.

We will update this policy if a later beta changes how it handles data. The date at the top shows when the text was last updated.
