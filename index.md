# Everyday Sudoku privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Everyday Sudoku (also called Puzzle in earlier TestFlight builds) is provided by **Navero App**. Navero App is responsible for the personal data it processes to operate the app. Contact us at [support@navero.app](mailto:support@navero.app).

This policy covers the iPhone app and its TestFlight versions. Advertising and purchase sections apply to versions in which those features are enabled. Earlier TestFlight builds without those features do not use them.

## Your game data

Your puzzles, saved games, results, XP, hint balance and preferences are stored on your device. We use these records to resume games, show progress and manage hints. The puzzle catalogue is included in the app. There is no player account or game-progress cloud sync, and we do not upload your Sudoku boards or move history to a Navero App server.

Removing the app and its local data removes these records from that installation. We do not hold a server copy of your progress. Device backups may retain copies according to your backup settings. Removing the app does not automatically delete purchase or advertising records held by service providers.

## Optional rewarded advertising

Versions with advertising use **Google AdMob** to offer an optional ad when you want to refill your hints. Ordinary puzzle play has no forced ads. An active Unlimited Hints purchase bypasses this ad flow.

Google's advertising services may process your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash or performance information. This supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. The advertising identifier on iOS is subject to Apple's tracking permission. See [Google's explanation of data used by partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Google and the advertising partners identified in the consent message may receive data needed to provide ads. The message provides the applicable partner list, purposes and choices. Google's consent service processes information about the app, device and your choices to determine which privacy messages and ad requests are permitted.

## Advertising choices

Where required, we request consent before advertising processing that needs it. You can review the available choices through **Settings → Ad privacy choices** in an advertising-enabled version. You can also change Apple's tracking permission in **iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

You may refuse or withdraw consent. Refusing personalized advertising or tracking does not necessarily prevent all advertising data processing: limited ads may still be available, depending on your choices and region. If no ad is available, you can continue playing Sudoku and use your daily hint allowance. An ad refill is granted only after the ad's reward conditions are met.

## Purchases and paid access

Apple processes in-app payments. We use **RevenueCat** to check purchase status and manage Unlimited Hints access. RevenueCat processes purchase and transaction records, a generated app-user identifier, and technical information such as app, device, operating-system and locale information. Purchase information also supports revenue reporting and fraud prevention.

Purchase services can be contacted to load an offer or check access even if you do not buy anything. We do not supply a player name or email address to RevenueCat, and we do not receive your full payment-card details from Apple. RevenueCat processes app customer data on our behalf under its [Data Processing Addendum](https://www.revenuecat.com/dpa). See also [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).

## TestFlight and support

When you use TestFlight, Apple collects testing information and shares crash reports, usage, device and build details with us. Email invitations may also make your name and email address available to us. Feedback can include comments and screenshots you choose to send. We use this information to investigate beta issues. Apple's handling is described in its [TestFlight privacy notice](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/).

If you email support@navero.app, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. This inbox is shared across Navero App products. We use messages to answer requests and identify recurring issues. Please avoid sending information you do not want us to receive in screenshots or attachments.

## Why we process information

Where European data-protection law applies, we rely on providing the requested service for purchase and paid-access administration; our legitimate interests in answering support requests, investigating problems and preventing fraud; and consent for advertising processing that requires it. Applicable advertising purposes and legal bases are described in the consent message. We may also retain or disclose records where required by law. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

## Retention and deletion

Local gameplay records remain on your device until removed. We delete TestFlight reports or screenshots we download when they are no longer needed to investigate beta issues. We keep support messages to answer requests and identify recurring issues, and delete messages from our support inbox when you ask us to.

Purchase records are kept as needed to administer and verify paid access, handle transaction issues and meet applicable legal obligations. Advertising and consent records are retained according to their purpose and the relevant providers' retention practices. Apple, Google and GitHub also manage records for their own services under their policies; deleting a support message does not delete those records.

For a request about data we control, including purchase-service records processed on our behalf, email [support@navero.app](mailto:support@navero.app). We may need information to locate the records and verify the request. We will explain any retention required by law or limitation that applies.

## Service providers and international processing

We use the providers described above and their service providers for purchases, advertising, testing and support. Data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. RevenueCat's data-processing terms provide contractual safeguards for applicable international transfers. Google and Apple describe their transfer arrangements in their privacy policies. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to data we control.

This policy page is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. Visiting it sends normal web-request information, such as your IP address, to GitHub. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Your rights and policy changes

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact [support@navero.app](mailto:support@navero.app) to exercise rights relating to data we control.

We will update this policy when our data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Where a change requires fresh consent, we will request it through the relevant consent flow.
