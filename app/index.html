Room to think

One grid. Your way to play.

Find your level.

Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert. A quick puzzle or a longer challenge, whenever you have a moment.

Choose your assistance.

Use Relaxed, Classic, Challenge or Custom settings. Pencil notes, undo and explainable hints help you find your way.

Keep your place.

Resume your puzzle, revisit recent results and see how each solve earned its XP. Your puzzle history stays on your device.

A little help, when you choose.

Every puzzle can be played offline. Three free hints return each day. If you run out, you can choose to watch an ad to refill three hints, or buy Unlimited Hints once.

Ordinary play has no forced ads. Ads, purchases and restoring purchases need an internet connection. No subscription or player account is required.
